// OPTIONAL: SHARE IT TO THE BOARD

The report is yours either way. Sharing it does two things.

Your profile gains a comparison against the unnamed cohort in your workshop room — computed only after eight people have shared, never from fewer. And it sits beside your session counts on the board, where the room can see what the counts alone could not say.

Sharing is a separate command, gated on a typed word, from the machine that ran the assessment:

python3 tokens.py --submit ~/Desktop/project-fit-*.html

Not on the board yet? Join from your laptop first. Remove everything any time with python3 tokens.py --leave.
